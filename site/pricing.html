FAQ

Pricing questions.

Is DietApp really free?

Yes. Self-hosted is free and open source. You only pay for the AI you use — or nothing with local models.

What's included in Cloud?

Can I switch from self-hosted to Cloud?

Yes. Export your database and import it into Cloud.

Do you offer refunds?

Full refund within 30 days, no questions asked.

All major cards, PayPal, and invoices for enterprise.

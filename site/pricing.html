AI Provider Costs

What you'll pay for AI

DietApp works with multiple AI providers. Here's what they cost.

Ollama

Free

Run AI models locally on your machine. No API keys, no costs, complete privacy.

  • Cost: $0
  • Models: Llama 3, Mistral, Qwen, and more
  • Requirements: 8GB+ RAM recommended
  • Best for: Privacy-focused users, offline use

LM Studio

Free

Desktop app for running local AI models with a friendly GUI.

  • Cost: $0
  • Models: Any GGUF format model
  • Requirements: 8GB+ RAM recommended
  • Best for: Users who prefer a GUI over CLI

OpenRouter

Pay-as-you-go

One API key, access to hundreds of models from multiple providers.

  • Cost: ~$0.01-0.10 per conversation
  • Models: Claude, GPT-4, Llama, and 100+ more
  • Requirements: API key from openrouter.ai
  • Best for: Flexibility, trying different models

OpenAI

Pay-as-you-go

Direct access to GPT-4o and other OpenAI models.

  • Cost: ~$0.05-0.15 per conversation
  • Models: GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini, o1-preview
  • Requirements: API key from platform.openai.com
  • Best for: Best-in-class performance

Typical usage: Most users spend $2-10/month on AI costs with cloud providers. Local providers (Ollama, LM Studio) are completely free but require more RAM.

FAQ

Pricing questions

Is DietApp really free?

Yes! The self-hosted version is completely free and open source. You only pay for the AI provider you choose to use (or $0 if you run local models like Ollama).

What's included in managed hosting?

We handle all the infrastructure: hosting, updates, backups, SSL certificates, and monitoring. You just sign up and start using DietApp. Your data is stored securely and backed up daily.

Can I switch from self-hosted to managed?

Absolutely. You can export your SQLite database from your self-hosted instance and import it into a managed account. We provide migration tools to make this seamless.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. If you're not satisfied with managed hosting, we offer a full refund within 30 days. No questions asked.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum). Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice.

Is there a discount for nonprofits or students?

Yes! We offer 50% off managed hosting for verified nonprofits and students. Contact us with proof of status to get your discount code.

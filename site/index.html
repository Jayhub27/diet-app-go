FAQ

Common questions.

Who is DietApp for?

Anyone with a restrictive diet — allergies, intolerances, medical diets — or anyone cooking for them.

Does it handle serious allergies?

Every recipe, suggestion, and scanned label is checked against your blacklist. For life-threatening allergies, still double-check labels yourself.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything stays in one file on your machine. With a local model, nothing leaves your device at all.

Can I run it fully offline?

Yes. Point it at Ollama or LM Studio. No internet, no account.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT forgets you. DietApp remembers your restrictions and checks every answer against them.

What does it cost?

Free to self-host. $5/month for the hosted cloud version. See pricing.

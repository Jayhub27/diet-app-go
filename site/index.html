Features

Everything you need to eat better.

No subscriptions, no cloud lock-in. One Go binary, one SQLite file, and an AI model of your choice.

💬

Conversational AI

Ask questions in plain language. DietApp streams replies token-by-token and calls tools behind the scenes to compute nutrition, search ingredients, and generate recipes.

🛒

Smart pantry

Track what you have at home. The AI uses your pantry when suggesting meals, so you cook with what you already own instead of buying more.

📖

Recipe library

Every recipe the AI generates can be saved. Full ingredients, step-by-step instructions, macros per serving, and dietary tags — all yours to keep.

⚠️

Allergy blacklist

Tell DietApp what you can’t eat once. It remembers forever and cross-checks every recipe, every suggestion, every grocery list.

📷

Vision mode

Snap a photo of your fridge or a restaurant menu. With local vision models, DietApp identifies ingredients and suggests what to cook or order.

📅

Weekly meal plans

Ask for a week of lunches, a high-protein plan, or a low-carb rotation. DietApp builds it, respects your restrictions, and generates the grocery list.

How it works

From zero to meal plan in three steps.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you can’t eat

    Type a sentence like “I’m allergic to peanuts and shellfish, and I hate cilantro.” The AI extracts every ingredient and adds it to your blacklist automatically.

  2. 2

    Stock your pantry

    Add what you have at home. DietApp will prioritise recipes that use those ingredients, reducing waste and grocery bills.

  3. 3

    Chat your way to dinner

    Ask for recipes, nutrition breakdowns, weekly plans, or grocery lists. Every answer is grounded in your data — never generic.

Bring your own model

Works with the AI you already trust.

DietApp speaks the OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions API, so it plugs into almost any provider. Run it fully offline with a local model, or point it at a cloud API — your choice.

  • OpenAI — GPT-4o and friends, with your own API key.
  • OpenRouter — one key, hundreds of models.
  • Ollama — run Llama, Mistral, or Qwen locally.
  • LM Studio — drag-and-drop local models with a GUI.
  • Local manager — DietApp can download and run curated models for you, including vision.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything lives in a single SQLite file on your machine. Nothing is sent anywhere except the AI provider you choose to use.

Can I run it fully offline?

Absolutely. Point DietApp at Ollama or LM Studio, or use the built-in local model manager to download a curated model. No internet required after setup.

Does it handle serious allergies?

DietApp cross-checks every recipe against your blacklist and dietary restrictions. For life-threatening allergies, always double-check labels — the AI is an assistant, not a medical device.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT forgets you between sessions. DietApp persists your pantry, restrictions, blacklist, and saved recipes, and uses them as context in every conversation.

Is there a mobile app?

DietApp is a responsive web app. Run the binary on any server (a Raspberry Pi, a VPS, your laptop) and open it from your phone’s browser.

What does it cost?

The app itself is free and open source. You pay only for the AI provider you use — or nothing if you run a local model.

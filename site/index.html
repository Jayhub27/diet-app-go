Before you download.

Does it replace my doctor or dietitian?

No. It checks ingredients against your rules — not medical advice.

Does it work offline?

Yes, with a local model. Cloud models need a connection.

Do I need an account?

No for the self-hosted app. The web app signs you in to sync.

What does it cost?

Self-hosting is free. The web app has a free tier; AI usage is billed by your provider.

Before you download.

Does it replace my doctor or dietitian?

No. DietApp checks ingredients against the rules you set. It doesn't diagnose, prescribe, or replace medical advice.

Does it work offline?

Yes, with a local model such as Ollama or LM Studio. Cloud models need a connection; the restriction checks run either way.

Do I need an account?

The self-hosted app doesn't ask for one — everything lives in a SQLite file on your machine. The hosted web app signs you in to sync across devices.

What does it cost?

Self-hosting is free forever under the MIT licence. The hosted web app has a free tier; cloud AI usage is optional and billed by your provider.

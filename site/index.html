Why DietApp

Built for restrictive diets, not generic recipe browsing.

Most food apps assume anything goes. DietApp assumes nothing — every recipe, suggestion, and grocery list is filtered through what you can't eat.

Real-time label checks

Point the camera at a product label. DietApp reads the ingredient list and flags anything on your blacklist — before it reaches your cart.

Your blacklist, everywhere

Allergies, intolerances, dislikes, medical diets. Tell DietApp once — every recipe, plan, and suggestion is checked against it from then on.

AI meal planning

Ask for a week of safe dinners. The AI plans it around your restrictions and writes the grocery list — the meal-prep toll, handed over.

Shopping guidance

Get the exact ingredients to buy, and what's on the product before you buy it. The generated list is built from recipes that are already safe for you.

See what's in the fridge

Snap your fridge and get dinner ideas from what's actually inside — still checked against your restrictions. Works with local vision models.

A library you can trust

Saved recipes carry their dietary tags, macros, and a verified blacklist check — so trust is built in, not remembered.

A calm, quiet interface

One question, one composer, no clutter. Built to be used one-handed at the stove or standing in a store aisle.

Native Android client

Scan labels in the store, chat from the kitchen. The APK connects to the server you run, on your network or over a tunnel.

How it works

From restrictions to dinner in three steps.

  1. 1

    Tell it what you can't eat

    Type "I'm allergic to peanuts and shellfish, and I hate cilantro." The AI extracts every ingredient into your blacklist automatically.

  2. 2

    Scan and ask while you shop

    Photograph a label or ask about a menu in plain language. Every answer comes checked against your blacklist, in the aisle, before you commit.

  3. 3

    Get a plan you don't have to double-check

    Weekly meals, safe recipes, and an exact grocery list — the whole planning toll handed to the AI, so dinner stops being a research project.

FAQ

Common questions.

Who is DietApp for?

People with restrictive diets: food allergies and intolerances, medical diets like low-sodium or low-FODMAP, and anyone who cooks for someone who does. If "what can I eat?" is a hard question, this is the app.

Does it handle serious allergies?

DietApp cross-checks every recipe, suggestion, and scanned label against your blacklist. For life-threatening allergies, always double-check labels yourself — DietApp is an assistant, not a medical device.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything — pantry, blacklist, recipes, plans — lives in a single SQLite file on your machine. There is no telemetry and no account, and nothing leaves your device except the requests you send to the AI provider you choose. With a local model, nothing leaves at all.

Can I run it fully offline?

Absolutely. Point DietApp at Ollama or LM Studio. No internet and no account required after setup — which also means it works in a supermarket with bad signal.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT forgets you between sessions and has never met your blacklist. DietApp persists your restrictions, dislikes, and saved recipes, and grounds every single answer in them.

What does it cost?

The source is open — audit it, self-host it, and with local AI you pay nothing at all. The cloud version is $5/month if you'd rather not run a server. See pricing.
